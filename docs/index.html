Jimmy

A personal health and habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. Fast, log, measure — and keep every bit of it to yourself.

View on GitHub Privacy policy

Fasting

A live timer for the fast you're in, a history of the ones you've finished, and a calendar that fills itself in — 16 hours or more completes the day.

Workouts & exercises

Log gym days at a glance, or go deeper and track individual exercises with sets, reps, and weight.

Drinks & sauna

Simple daily calendars for the habits you just want to see a streak of.

Body measurements

Track any measurement you care about and watch it move over time.

Apple Health

Reads your weight and step count — with your permission — so trends and weekly averages sit right next to your habits. Jimmy never writes back.

Judgement Day

An optional countdown to a date of your choosing, for when a deadline is the thing that actually works.

Your data never leaves your control

Everything lives on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud database. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, no accounts — and no servers on our end to put any of it on. Read the privacy policy.

Built with SwiftUI and SwiftData. Requires iOS 18.2 or later.
