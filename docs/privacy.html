Jimmy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Jimmy is a personal health and habit tracking app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We never see it.

Data the app stores

Jimmy stores the information you enter in the app — workouts, exercises, fasting sessions, sauna and drink tracking, and body measurements.

This data is kept:

  • On your device, and
  • In your private iCloud database (CloudKit), if you are signed in to iCloud, so it can sync between your devices.

Your private iCloud database is controlled by your Apple ID. The developer of Jimmy has no access to it and cannot read, share, or sell anything in it.

If you use the export feature, a copy of your workout data is written to your own iCloud Drive, where it remains under your control.

Apple Health

With your permission, Jimmy reads your weight and step count from Apple Health to show trends and weekly averages alongside your tracked habits.

  • Health data is read on your device and displayed to you. It is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party.
  • Jimmy does not write any data to Apple Health.
  • You can revoke this access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Jimmy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising
  • No third-party services or SDKs that receive your data
  • No accounts, and no data collected by the developer — we operate no servers of our own

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove synced data from iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete the data stored by Jimmy.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.
